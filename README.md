## 1. Problem Statement

Food waste is a challenge that affects food security and resource management. Restaurants, bakeries, shops and markets may have surplus food that is still safe for consumption, while some community organizations struggle to access sufficient food for the people they support.

FoodLink Rwanda aims to address this problem by connecting food donors with organizations that can redistribute surplus food.

## 2. Project Objectives

### Main Objective
To develop an Oracle database system that facilitates the management and redistribution of surplus food in Rwanda.

### Specific Objectives
- To register food donors and community organizations.
- To manage surplus food donations and inventory.
- To manage food requests and approvals.
- To track food collection and distribution.
- To reduce food waste and support food redistribution.

## 3. Planned Data Sources

- FAO Food Loss and Waste Database.
- National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR).
- Additional operational records collected from relevant food donors and community organizations.

## 4. Planned Database Tables

- DONORS
- ORGANIZATIONS
- FOOD_CATEGORIES
- FOOD_ITEMS
- DONATIONS
- FOOD_REQUESTS
- DISTRIBUTIONS

## 5. Planned PL/SQL Features

- Procedures for registering donations and processing requests.
- Functions for calculating available food quantities.
- Cursors for generating distribution reports.
- Packages for organizing business operations.
- Triggers for enforcing business rules.
- Exception handling for invalid transactions.
- Audit logs for tracking database activities.

## 6. Planned Innovation

A web-based interactive application developed using Python Streamlit and connected to Oracle 21c.

The application will provide donation management, inventory tracking, food request processing, distribution monitoring and expiry notifications.
