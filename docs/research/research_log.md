### Source 1: World Bank (2020)

Title: Rwanda Food Smart – Country Diagnostic

Organization: World Bank

Link: https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/288911601302842762/pdf/Rwanda-Food-Smart-Country-Diagnostic.pdf

Key Findings:
The report identifies food loss and waste as major
challenges in Rwanda. It estimates that approximately
40% of total food production is lost or wasted.
Tomatoes, maize and rice experience significant losses.
Poor storage, handling and transportation contribute
to these challenges.

Relevance to FoodLink:
This research supports the need for a food redistribution
system. It provides justification for donation management,
inventory tracking and food distribution.

Research Gap:
The report discusses food loss at a national and supply
chain level. FoodLink will focus on managing individual
donations and connecting donors with recipient organizations.

### Source 2: NISR (2024)

Title: Comprehensive Food Security and Vulnerability
Analysis (CFSVA) 2024

Organization: National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda

Link: https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2025-07/Rwanda%20CFSVA%202024.pdf

Key Findings:
The survey covered 9,000 households across Rwanda's
30 districts. It found that 83% of households were
food secure, 16% moderately food insecure and
1% severely food insecure.

Relevance to FoodLink:
This research provides evidence about food security
challenges in Rwanda. It supports the importance of
improving food access through organized food redistribution.

Research Gap:
The survey focuses on household food security.
FoodLink will focus on the operational management
of food donations, inventory and distribution.

### Source 3: UNEP (2024)

Title: Food Waste Index Report 2024

Organization: United Nations Environment Programme

Link: https://wedocs.unep.org/handle/20.500.11822/45230

Key Findings:
The report estimates that approximately 1.05 billion
tonnes of food were wasted worldwide in 2022.
Households accounted for 60%, food services for 28%,
and retail businesses for 12% of the total.

Relevance to FoodLink:
The research highlights food waste in restaurants
and retail businesses, which are potential food donors
in the FoodLink system.

Research Gap:
The report examines global food waste.
FoodLink will focus on managing safe surplus food
donations and redistributing them to local organizations
in Rwanda.
